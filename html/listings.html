Our standard

What “verified” means on Stackly

Before a property appears here, our team confirms the six points below. Ask for the full information pack on any listing.

Ownership and title

The seller or landlord's right to lease or sell is confirmed against records.

Zoning and approvals

Permitted use, FSI and building approvals are checked for the intended purpose.

Safety and compliance

Fire, occupancy and utility certifications are reviewed and noted.

Lease and income records

For let properties, tenancy terms and rental income are matched to documents.

Site inspection

An advisor visits the property so photos and specifications reflect reality.

Price benchmarking

Asking rents and prices are compared with similar properties in the micro-market.

Lease or buy

Which route suits your business?

Both are available across our listings. Here is how they differ in practice.

Leasing

Best for occupiers who want flexibility and lower upfront cost.

  • Capital stays in the business. You pay monthly rent instead of a purchase price.
  • Room to grow. Lock-in periods and expansion options can be negotiated.
  • Faster move-in. Managed and furnished spaces can be ready quickly.
  • Running costs vary. Maintenance and escalations depend on the lease terms.

Buying

Best for investors and long-term owners building asset value.

  • Asset ownership. You hold the property and any appreciation in its value.
  • Rental income. Let properties show their yield so you can compare returns.
  • Larger commitment. Due diligence, funding and registration take more time.
  • Exit planning. Tenant quality and location affect how easily you can resell.

Contact an advisor
Are the listed prices final?

No. Prices are indicative and subject to negotiation. We confirm current terms with the owner when you enquire.

What do I receive when I enquire?

An advisor sends the full information pack for the property, including specifications, documents and commercial terms, and can arrange a visit.

Can I see properties that are not listed here?

Yes. We also work with off-market inventory. Share your requirements and we will include suitable options in your shortlist.

How is the yield on sale listings calculated?

Yield reflects annual rental income relative to the asking price. Ask for the rent roll in the information pack to see the working behind it.

Do you help after I choose a property?

Yes. We support negotiation, legal and documentation coordination, and handover through to completion.
