Before you reach out

Help us help you faster

You do not need everything ready. A few details just let us give you sharper recommendations on the very first call.

  • Preferred cities or micro-markets
  • Approximate size in square feet and headcount
  • Budget range, or the rent and price you have in mind
  • Move-in or transaction timeline
  • Lease, buy, sell or invest, and any must-haves
