Questions

Common questions about commercial property

Can't find your answer? Our team replies within one working day.

Ask an advisor
Do you handle both leasing and buying?

Yes. We advise occupiers on leases and investors on purchases and sales across all eight asset classes listed above.

What lease terms are typical for offices?

Terms vary by building and operator. Managed offices often offer short lock-ins, while full-floor leases in Grade-A towers usually run longer with scheduled escalations. We will explain the options for your shortlist.

Can you help with land that has no approvals yet?

We map zoning, FSI and approval status before you commit, and will tell you clearly what is in place and what is still pending.

Is there a fee for the first consultation?

No. The 30-minute consultation and initial shortlist are free and carry no obligation.

How soon can I view a property?

Once you pick from the shortlist, we usually arrange visits within a few working days, subject to owner availability.
